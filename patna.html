Patna

Overview

The modern capital with an ancient soul. Once known as Patliputra, it was the powerhouse that ruled much of the Indian subcontinent under the Mauryan and Gupta empires.

Things to Do

  • Climb the Golghar for a panoramic city view
  • Spend a day at the world-class Bihar Museum
  • Enjoy a boat ride on the Ganges
  • Visit Takht Sri Patna Sahib

Travel Tips

The city is well-connected by air, rail, and road. Utilize local cabs and auto-rickshaws for easy city commuting.
